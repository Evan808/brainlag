Brain Lag

Answer the problem from N steps ago.

How to play
  1. A new problem shows up at the top. Don't answer it yet — remember it.
  2. Once N more problems have appeared, answer that old one at the bottom. Its numbers are hidden.
  3. Problems in between are covered. You're holding N answers in your head at once.
  4. Type with the keypad or your keyboard. 20 answers per round.
